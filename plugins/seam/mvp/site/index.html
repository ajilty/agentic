Useful and auditable — you don't have to choose.

The point of Seam is that a security-conscious person gets both: a triage that actually helps, and the ability to see exactly what it saw, decided, and did. Some guarantees hold in code today; others are designed and honestly labelled. We don't badge what we haven't built.

enforced today

You approve every write. This build holds no write capability at all. When drafting arrives it stages for your confirmation and never sends, posts, or notifies anyone.

enforced today

Every event names its actor. user or agent, on every state change — including every model call. "What did it do while I was away" is answerable line by line. (Plaintext today; tamper-evidence is planned.)

enforced today

Work and personal never mix. Seam refuses to start against a store belonging to another profile.

designed

Synced text is data, never instructions. The posture is settled; the compiler that must honour it isn't built yet, so we don't yet claim it as enforced.

designed

External urgency is marked and capped. Visible in the board today; the tiering code and its test are the next milestone.

designed

Rules never come from message bodies. The poisoned-message fixture exists; adjudication and injection-logging are specified, not yet wired.

What leaves your machine

We won't tell you nothing leaves — that would be the claim that fails an audit. Your files stay local; inference does not. To compile a card, the relevant message text goes to a model provider, and every such call is logged.

Stays local

Raw messages, workstreams, facts, the ledger, everything Seam learns. Plain files under a folder you chose. No server, no account, no telemetry.

Goes to the model provider

The message text needed to compile a card — per compilation, nothing else. Recorded as a model_call ledger event with provider, versioned model id, message ids, and bytes sent. What the provider retains or trains on is disclosed from their terms in the docs — not asserted here.

Who it is for

One person — specifically, a security-conscious leader who owes answers and audits their tools.

The person who owes answers and trusts nothing by default

Your day is other people's asks, and the expensive miss is forgetting a person, not a task. You'd also want to read the source, the ledger, and the test suite before letting anything near your mailbox. Seam is built for exactly that person: it helps, and it shows its work. It is not a team product and has no multi-user story — n=1 is the design, not a gap.

What it can't do yet

  • It cannot send, reply, post, or write anything — drafts included. Writes arrive later, always user-confirmed; sending never does.
  • Mail and calendar only. Chat is not connected in this release.
  • No drift detection against a tracker — that needs access it doesn't have.
  • Reflect (rollups) and Know (facts) are designed, not built. Operate is the whole of this release.
  • Day one it knows nothing about you until the 10-minute setup conversation seeds it.
  • Personal-first: dogfooded on real mail before it ships anywhere, likely open-sourced, not sold.
